---
title: Roadmap - The Corvus Guild
---
To be a sustainable organization, The Corvus Guild will be prioritizing a cycle that closely matches the heartbeat of international tech event cycles. We promote a deliberate pacing. Velocity is good, **but psychological safety matters more to us more**. The Corvus Guild is aware it is founded in the middle of international sprints. While it is tempting to immediately join these events and show representation, The Corvus Guild treats preparation, provisions, and morale as a priority that must be fulfilled to ensure a smooth journey along the way.

## Q3 & Q4 2026

Q3 and Q4 of 2026 will be allocated for getting The Corvus Guild on its feet. We will prioritize building the necessary infrastructure and channels to foster the appropriate environment for our members. As of June 2026, we are focusing our efforts into building our presence, handling administrative work, and reaching out with local partners. We are also using this time for the acquisition of members. The latter half will be utilized to fast-track members with the necessary introductions and training to prepare them for the coming quarters.

## Q1 2027

As a new fiscal year starts, we will be utilizing Q1 for getting our student developers well-adjusted and comfortable with the culture advocated by The Corvus Guild. We will continue fast-tracking members, especially newer ones. By this period, the core team of The Corvus Guild as well as voluntary participants will be building in public and showcasing their projects. The idea is to ship code and publicize them as templates for other members to tinker. By doing this, The Corvus Guild will show that anyone can join international hackathons, that things are not as intimidating as they seem. By setting up examples and documenting our process, we hope to show that  anyone can get in on the action.

## Q2 2027 and onwards

Moving to Q2 of 2027, we encourage members to form groups and participate in online international events and hackathons. This way, our participants can develop the confidence to build in public, produce working artifacts, manage their own time, and acquire the skillset with the latest technology stack. As for The Corvus Guild's part, we will continue curating examples and showcasing our members' work. We will be responsible for informing members with the latest events and opportunities.
